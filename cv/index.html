Professional Summary

I am a Ph.D. student in privacy-preserving decentralized learning at INRIA Rennes. My research focuses on privacy techniques for decentralized learning, with a particular emphasis on noise correlation. I work on both theory and practice to design approaches that are scalable, tractable, and supported by strong privacy guarantees.
