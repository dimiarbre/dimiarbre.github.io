## Course overview

This course is aimed at Master 1 students at the Cyberschool in Rennes. It explores the different ways information can be tracked, and how privacy can be preserved, across a range of systems.

As part of this course, I also designed and taught a dedicated session on Local Differential Privacy, covering its theoretical foundations and practical applications, followed by a hands-on practical session.
