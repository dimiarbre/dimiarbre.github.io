## Course overview

This course is aimed at Master 1 students in the University of Rennes' [AI class](https://istic.univ-rennes.fr/master-intelligence-artificielle-parcours-ia-et-applications). I gave both theoretical and practical sessions, during the first iteration of this master's program.

The course is divided into three parts: privacy, explainable AI, and attacks on AI models.
